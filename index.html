Add song from URL

Paste a link to a page with the song's text. SetBook fetches it, pulls out the text lines, and opens a mass-entry section where you split the text into sections, tidy it up, then create the sections.

SetBook couldn't read the text from that site. Use the SetBook scraper extension instead: open the lyrics page, click the extension's toolbar button, and it sends the text straight into SetBook.

The site must allow cross-origin reads — plain-text and raw-file URLs work best. Only http(s) addresses are fetched.

        SetBook Help

        Last updated 2026-10-01.

        1. Your songbook 2. Finding songs 3. Writing charts 4. Checking your work 5. Making PDFs 6. Saving & backups 7. Songs from the web 8. On your devices

        1. Your songbook

        SetBook keeps your songs as chord charts — chords sitting right above the lyrics where they change, organized the way musicians think: by section. Verse, Chorus, Bridge, and your own names. Build your library once, then read it on any screen or print it for the music stand.

        Everything lives in a file you own, on your device or in your Google Drive. No account, no server, no subscription — your songs are yours.

        2. Finding songs

        • Search matches titles, artists, tags, chords, and lyric lines — it shows you the matching line in each section.
        • Sort by title or artist; filter by Cover/Original, tags, or artist. The Sort & Filter panel tucks these away so the list gets the room.
        • Ready status: every song carries a badge — Not Ready, In Progress, or Ready. Tap the badge to move a song along as you learn it, then filter to Ready only when the gig's coming.
        • Focus (◎): tap it on a song to hide everything else and work on just that song. “← All songs” brings the list back.
        • Details (the ⓘ icon on each song): a full page for the song's title, artists (one per line), tags, type, ready status, and video links — album version and live video, each with a Watch button.
        • On phones the list becomes a drawer — tap the menu button; picking a song hands the full screen to the chart.

        3. Writing charts

        • Each section is a free-text field. Type a line of chords, then the lyric line under it — chords show gold, lyrics white, so you can see at a glance what the app heard.
        • Sections: pick Intro, Verse, Chorus, Pre-Chorus, Bridge, Instrumental, Outro, or your own Custom name. Add a label like (guitar solo) after the name if you like. Numbering is automatic — two choruses become Chorus 1 and Chorus 2 on their own.
        • + Add section appends whatever naturally follows (a Verse earns a Chorus next, and so on). Blank lines carry Split chips to divide a section in two — the new section lands directly below the one you split. ↑ ↓ in a section's header moves it one slot up or down within the song.
        • If the app misreads a line, hover over or tap into its field to reveal the little chip on the line — it flips the reading: as lyrics on a chord line, as chords on a lyric line. A line you've overridden keeps a small auto chip so you can switch it back.
        • Transpose: tell the section what key it's written in, then step it up or down — the chord symbols move, everything else stays put. Your original chords are never changed, so it's always reversible.
        • Private notes: wrap a lyric line in /* … */ and it stays out of your PDFs.
        • Undo (File menu or Ctrl/Cmd+Z) covers clears, deletes, moves, and drags.
        • Prefer the classic row editor? File → Configuration… → Use mass-entry editor for section editing switches back.

        4. Checking your work

        • Section Preview: unfold the accordion under any section to see just that section's Performance PDF page.
        • Song preview: the PDF icon on a song header shows exactly what that song's PDF will look like — Performance pages swipe sideways like a page-turner; Print-Friendly scrolls vertically.

        5. Making PDFs

        • Performance PDF — one page per section, sized for your device (iPad, iPhone, Android, or paper), portrait or landscape, dark or light, with a tappable table of contents and section navigation. Made for a tablet on a music stand and Bluetooth page-turner pedals.
        • Print-Friendly PDF — a clean continuous document with page numbers, for paper.
        • Choose your songs: the whole library, a saved set, or tick them off yourself — Ready only grabs your gig-ready songs in one tap.
        • After generating, Share PDF… sends the file straight to Books, Mail, Messages, or wherever you keep your charts.

        6. Saving & backups

        • Your library saves automatically to the file you opened (Chrome/Edge), or on demand with Save as (Safari/Firefox).
        • Google Drive (File → Connect Google Drive…): keep the library in Drive and it syncs as you work — your phone, tablet, and computer stay in step.
        • Snapshots (File → Snapshots…): automatic backups inside the app. Compare versions, see what changed, restore any of them.
        • If the app ever closes unexpectedly, it offers to pick up where you left off when you return.
        • Back up now and then — Save as… or a downloaded copy. The Snapshots screen nudges you if it's been a while.
        • The Songs menu imports songs from another SetBook file (from your computer, or from Google Drive when connected), exports just the songs you're looking at, lists recently viewed songs, and recovers the last five songs you deleted. Imported songs arrive marked In Progress, so they're easy to find and review.

        7. Songs from the web

        • + Add song from URL: paste a link to a lyrics/chords page and SetBook pulls the text in as a new song, split into sections at [Verse]/[Chorus]-style headers automatically.
        • Some sites block that — the dialog will tell you. The SetBook Scraper extension (Chrome/Edge) covers those sites: open the page, click the extension's toolbar button, and the text lands in SetBook ready to import.
        • Install: download the zip from the dialog, unzip it into a folder you'll keep, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and pick that folder.
        • Tidy the imported text in the staging area, then Create sections — or Reset to start over from the original text.

        8. On your devices

        • Help → Getting PDFs onto your devices… walks through moving your PDFs to Apple Books (iPhone/iPad) or Files by Google (Android).
        • SetBook works offline once opened — add it to your home screen and your library, charts, and PDF export all work without a connection. (Google Drive syncing needs connectivity at save time.)
